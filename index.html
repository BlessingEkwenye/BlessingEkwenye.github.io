My Framework: Storytelling + Psychology + Strategy

I combine three powerful disciplines to create email sequences that move people from interest to action.

📖 Storytelling (AIDA)

Attention: Hook with narrative hooks, not generic subject lines. Interest: Build curiosity through story beats. Desire: Connect story to transformation. Action: Clear, irresistible CTA.

🧠 Psychology

Leverage reciprocity, social proof, scarcity, and authority. Use pattern interrupts. Tap into pain points before presenting solutions. Make people feel understood before asking for commitment.

📊 Strategy (MOFU)

Middle of Funnel: Nurture awareness. Build trust. Establish credibility. Move subscribers closer to decision. Every email has a purpose in the journey.
